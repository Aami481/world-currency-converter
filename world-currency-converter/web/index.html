    How the conversion works

    Every rate is stored as units of that currency per 1 US dollar. Any pair goes through the dollar:

    result = amount ÷ rate_from × rate_to

    One table of numbers supports every pair, so nothing is stored per pair.

    Where the rates come from

    On load the page fetches live mid-market rates from the open currency-api. If you are offline it uses the last rates it saved, and if there are none, the built-in snapshot of . A rate you edit always wins over both. These rates are indicative; do not use them for trading.

    Shortcuts

    Keys 1 to 5 switch tabs, S swaps the currencies, and / jumps to search. Amounts accept commas, like 1,500.75.
